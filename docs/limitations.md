# Limitations

> **LANE:** Public limitations  
> **IS:** Required constraints on interpretation and use  
> **IS NOT:** An exhaustive risk assessment  
> **FORBIDDEN CROSS-OVERS:** Claims of guaranteed safety, correctness, compliance, or autonomy  
> **SOURCE OF TRUTH:** Observable behavior of the tagged public release  
> **GATE:** Limitations must remain visible beside release claims

- The harness depends on the quality and freshness of the authority sources it reads.
- A verifier can test the wrong invariant or fail to observe an important external condition.
- Receipts record declared evidence; they do not make weak evidence strong.
- Human authorization can be ambiguous, mistaken, or broader than intended.
- The reference skill cannot enforce permissions outside the host environment.
- Tool side effects vary across environments and must be classified locally.
- The synthetic fixture demonstrates decision behavior, not production reliability.
- The public extraction has not yet earned third-party compatibility or measured outcome claims.
- Cybernetic terminology describes the control structure; it does not prove system stability.
