## Scope

This repository is an independently written public extraction of operating patterns James uses in his personal AI stack: selective context, authority checks, state sensing, bounded action, verification, receipts, and rule improvement.

It excludes private source files, product contracts, deployment identities, credentials, client material, incident records, private prompts, raw traces, and operational data. The demonstration uses invented project names and values.

Automated hygiene checks cannot establish ownership or confidentiality by themselves. Human review remains mandatory.
